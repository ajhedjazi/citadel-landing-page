The Rebirth Philosophy

The goal isn’t another restart.

People are surrounded by diets, influencers and advice that contradicts itself. More information is rarely the real answer.

Rebirth brings the focus back to the individual: your goals, your lifestyle and the reasons progress has not lasted before.

Good coaching creates understanding, so the decisions become yours and the support eventually becomes unnecessary.

Your progress should become something you own—not something you rent.

Meet the Founder

Get to know Phil.

Phil’s own experience with weight loss shaped the way he coaches.

Rebirth rejects dependency-based coaching. The aim is to help people understand what works for them, build standards they respect and develop habits that can survive real life.

Coaching should empower people to continue independently. It is a process of changing the body, but also identity, decisions and the way you show up when nobody is watching.
