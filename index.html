The Rebirth Philosophy

Meet the Founder

Get to know Phil.

Phil’s own experience with weight loss shaped the way he coaches.

Rebirth rejects dependency-based coaching. The aim is to help people understand what works for them, build standards they respect and develop habits that can survive real life.

Coaching should empower people to continue independently. It is a process of changing the body, but also identity, decisions and the way you show up when nobody is watching.
