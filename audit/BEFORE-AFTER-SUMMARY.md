# Before / After Summary

## What Was Wrong

The site was fast, but it was asking visitors to understand the philosophy before it clearly explained the offer.

The live baseline also had a few concrete technical issues:

- The canonical pointed to a placeholder domain.
- The sitemap was missing.
- Social sharing metadata was missing.
- Structured data was missing.
- Lighthouse found a colour contrast issue on small orange labels.

## What Changed

The homepage now uses a simpler conversion-focused structure:

1. Hero
2. Reality Check
3. Ways to Work
4. How Coaching Works
5. Real Progress
6. Coach & Philosophy
7. Final enquiry CTA

The first screen now says what Rebirth Arete helps with:

- getting fitter
- losing weight
- building strength
- feeling better physically
- choosing between 1-to-1 PT and online coaching

The repeated restart/consistency messaging has been merged into one stronger section. The philosophy is still there, but it now supports the coaching offer instead of delaying it.

## Proof Stayed Honest

No fake testimonials, fake transformations, fake app screenshots, fake credentials, fake locations, or fake results were added.

The Real Progress section uses truthful progress markers for now, and the structure can accept genuine proof assets later.
